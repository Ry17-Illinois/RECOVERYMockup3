_title: James's Vocabulary of Happiness
description: A word-frequency dataset counting happiness-related terms across twelve works and collections by William James, dated 1890 to 1909, with undated letters and collected volumes. As displayed, the four forms Happy, Happier, Happiest, and Happiness are combined into a single column, "Happ(y/ier/iest/iness)," alongside Joy, Joyous, Bliss, Delight, Pleasure, and Pain. The downloadable CSV preserves the four forms as separate columns. The counts show that happiness language peaks in The Varieties of Religious Experience (1902), while the broader psychological vocabulary of "Pleasure" and "Pain" dominates the earlier Principles of Psychology (1890).
